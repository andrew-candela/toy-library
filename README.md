A way for folks to advertise toys they'd like to give away,
and to express interest in toys the'd like to borrow.

## Value Prop

This app is meant to encourage families to share toys. It needs to exist because not enough people put the toys their kids are done with on sites like buy-nothing etc. If you do that, you are a hero. Some people put the toys in boxes on a shelf or something - good for you if you do this. Others put them in a pile in a corner (no judgement - I have a pile like this). Sometimes toys go in the trash... not great, but I understand the appeal.

Almost all toys have a short half-life of entertainment value. Most probably offer 99% of their value in a few weeks - the same amount of time you'd typically keep a libray book. Some are loved, of course - keep those. We're talking about toys that you trip over more often than they get played with. I believe that children would be healthier and happier if they had a pipeline of new toys coming in and - importantly - old toys going out.

The toy library encourages both pipelines. When you give a toy away it stays in the library. There's no need for the next owner to take photos and post etc. When they are ready to give it up they select someone who has expressed interest in it and it moves on to the next owner. If your kid loves a toy that you got from the library and you both never want to give it up... that's great! Keep it forever by deleting it from your list of toys in the library. You are free to do this and it would be a great outcome. Either way we're keeping toys in the hands of someone who appreciates them, off of our floors and out of the landfill. Everyone wins.
